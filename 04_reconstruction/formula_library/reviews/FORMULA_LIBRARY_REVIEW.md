# Formula Library Review Notes

## Critic Rules Applied

- Formula extraction is not validation.
- A formula can be catalogued before it is trusted.
- Every formula must keep its provenance class.
- OCR text is only a working copy until image/PDF verification.
- Spreadsheet/code formulas may help reconstruct but cannot silently overwrite source formulas.
