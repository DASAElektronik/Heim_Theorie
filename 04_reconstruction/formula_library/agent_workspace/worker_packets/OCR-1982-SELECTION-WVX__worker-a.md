## Ambiguities

- The `q_x` glyphs in the structure-potency formulas are tightly set and can be confused with plain `qx` in OCR.
- The `A_{34}^{1-q_x}` term in the `w_{\nu x}` block is visually compressed; the exponent boundary is clear enough to transcribe, but not clean enough to treat as canonical.
- In `b_{\nu x}`, the grouping around the `kP(1+P+Q+\kappa\eta^2-q)` denominator and the `k^{-1}` factor is dense; I kept the visible structure but would not integrate it without a second pass.
- `A_{44}(-\alpha)^{2-k}A_{45}^{k-1}` is the likely reading, but the source line break makes the factorization easy to mistranscribe.
- The OCR `\vartheta` term in `A_{12}` is visually distinct enough to keep, but the symbol is dense and should be checked against the scan before canonical use.
