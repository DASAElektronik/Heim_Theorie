# Invariance rationale source review - 2026-09-06

Scope: bounded visual reconstruction of the early Volume-I A-minus/A-plus
framework relevant to the later E_k=pc use. Literal source statements are
separated from a matrix-algebra diagnostic. No formula or implementation is
changed.

## Result

The book supplies a stated route to E_Q=pc, but not an explicit comparison
with a rest-energy-subtracted kinetic energy. At Volume I p. 12 it moves
from its electromagnetic relativity principle and A-minus invariance to
E=mc^2, then writes the transverse-mass relation
m sqrt(1-beta^2)=m_0 and p=mv, followed by:

    "Hieraus folgt dann das relativistische Energieprinzip E_Q = pc."

It then equates E_Q with wave energy E_n=n h nu and uses nu lambda=c to
obtain p lambda=h. Thus the visible motivation is the book's claimed
Lorentz/invariance-plus-wave-quantization chain. It does not say that pc is
selected instead of conventional mechanical kinetic energy, define a
separate physical energy kind for that contrast, or state an active/passive
transformation convention in the bounded pages.

A literal matrix issue remains open. The printed p. 21 A-minus block, its
printed tan(psi-minus)=i beta-minus, and the claimed orthogonality must be
retained unchanged. Under ordinary complex trigonometry and ordinary
transpose multiplication, the claim does not follow. This is a reviewer
diagnostic, not a silent correction or an attributed authorial intent.

## Literal A-minus matrix and limited diagnostic

The visually checked p. 21 two-dimensional block is:

    [ cos(psi-minus)        i sin(psi-minus) ]
    [ -i sin(psi-minus)       cos(psi-minus) ],  tan(psi-minus)=i beta-minus.

The page calls it orthogonal. The companion A-plus block is the ordinary
real rotation pattern with cos(psi-plus), sin(psi-plus), -sin(psi-plus),
cos(psi-plus).

Diagnostic, not source transcription: if the printed T in the
orthogonality property is ordinary transpose, and sine/cosine use ordinary
complex analytic identities, the displayed A-minus block times its
transpose is

    (cos^2(psi-minus)-sin^2(psi-minus)) I.

With tan(psi-minus)=i beta-minus this is
[(1+beta-minus^2)/(1-beta-minus^2)] I, not I away from beta=0. The source
does not define a different transpose/product or a nonstandard
trigonometric convention in the inspected context.

The p. 56 comparison is not a source-authorized repair. Its matrix belongs
to R6, is 6 by 6, uses the 1-4 subblock as part of a different world-tensor
construction, and has no extra i on its off-diagonals. It is therefore
neither identical to nor a stated replacement for the p. 21 type-4
A-minus matrix. If ordinary complex trigonometry is applied to the p. 56
printed i beta=tan(psi) and cos^2+sin^2=1, one can infer
cos(psi)=1/sqrt(1-beta^2) and sin(psi)=i beta/sqrt(1-beta^2), up to branch
choice. Those gamma-like identities are not printed as an explicit p. 56
definition, and cannot repair p. 21 without an extra assumption.

## What the sources explain and leave open

- They state an electromagnetic-relativity/invariance argument, transverse
  mass and momentum relations, the asserted E_Q=pc, and an immediate
  wave-quantization/de-Broglie step. Later pages reuse pc as E_k.
- They do not state in this chain why E_Q=pc should be read as, or replace,
  a conventional rest-energy-subtracted kinetic energy. The E_Q/E_k name
  and repeated equations are positive source evidence; the pc-versus-T
  distinction is an external terminology/algebra check, not authorial
  intent.
- They do not give an active/passive convention, a derivation from the
  displayed p. 21 A-minus entries to the claimed orthogonality, or an early
  explicit rationale for a circular electron wave. The p. 12 wave argument
  is general quantization/de-Broglie reasoning, not a circular-electron-wave
  derivation.
- A bounded source search located the p. 12, p. 81, and p. 288 pc forms. No
  distinct, explicitly defined invariant-energy norm was found in the
  inspected A-minus chain. Formula OCR is degraded, so this is a bounded
  locator finding, not a corpus-wide absence claim.
