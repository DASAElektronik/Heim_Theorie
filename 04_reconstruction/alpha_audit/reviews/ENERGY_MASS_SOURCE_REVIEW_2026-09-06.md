# Energy, mass, and transverse-mass source review - 2026-09-06

Scope: bounded source reconstruction of `m(v_H)`, the invariant transverse
mass, and `E_k=m v_H c` immediately before Book II Eq. (105). This is an
evidence review only; no formula revision, fit, or validity claim is made.

## Result

Book II pp. 299-301 uses `E_k` as the kinetic component in its p-electron
correspondence-system construction:

```text
0 <= E <= E_k,       X + E = const,       p/e- rest masses excluded
v_H = c alpha > 0,   p = m v_H,           E_k = m v_H c = alpha m c^2.
```

The book expressly calls `E_k` kinetic energy and evaluates it as `pc`.
The inspected source chain does not print a comparison with a
rest-energy-subtracted expression such as `(gamma-1)m_0 c^2`. It therefore
establishes the repeated name and equation, plus the book's
`A_-`-invariant/transverse form; it does **not** itself state a contrast
with the conventional kinetic-energy convention. That contrast is our
subsequent terminology check, not an attributed authorial intention.

## Source chain and scope limit

```text
Book I p.81:  M sqrt(1-beta^2)=M_0; E_k=Mcv (=pc)
Book I p.288: m=m_0/sqrt(1-beta^2); E^2=p^2c^2; E_1=pc
                              |
Book II p.300: v_H=c alpha; E_k is called local kinetic energy
Book II p.301: p=m v_H; m(v_H); E_k=m v_H c=alpha m c^2
```

This is strong cross-volume continuity, but Book II pp. 299-301 do not print
a separate functional definition `m(v_H)=...`. Thus the Book-I mass law is
a source-reference anchor for the Book-II notation, not a newly proved
derivation of it.

## Scope of the “kinetic” wording

- The book itself calls `E_k=Mcv=pc` kinetic energy (I p. 81), calling the
  representation "bekannt", and repeats the construction under stated
  special-relativity premises (I p. 288). It calls `E_k` the kinetic energy
  of corpuscular e- (II p. 300).
- It also makes `E` only a component of a p/e- correspondence-system
  energy, with the rest masses explicitly excluded (II p. 299).
- The inspected pages do not contain `(gamma-1)m_0c^2` or compare it to
  `E_k`. That absence does not show that the author intended a new or
  nonstandard kinetic-energy concept. The distinction between `pc` and the
  conventional rest-energy-subtracted kinetic energy is an external
  algebraic/terminological check; it must be labelled as ours, rather than
  read back as an authorial claim.
- `X+E=const`, the correspondence interpretation, and the p. 301
  equality after the stated invariance are assumptions/algebra internal to
  the book, not independent empirical validation.

## Bounded negative findings

- Targeted OCR searches in the two local book texts for
  `Transversalmasse`, `m(v`, `M(v`, `v_H`, and the local energy notation
  located the Book-I transverse-mass anchors on pp. 81 and 288 and the
  Book-II p. 301 occurrence. No earlier explicit printed function
  definition named `m(v_H)` was located in that bounded search.
- The negative finding applies only to these search terms, local PDFs, and
  immediate Eq. (105) context; it is not a claim about the wider corpus.
